Demanding or giving dowry is illegal in India. Learn about the Dowry Prohibition Act, Section 498A (cruelty by husband/in-laws), and what constitutes dowry harassment under Indian law.

The Equal Remuneration Act guarantees women equal pay for equal work. Learn about discrimination protections in hiring, promotion, and working conditions under Indian labour law.

The Hindu Succession (Amendment) Act, 2005 gave daughters equal coparcenary rights. Learn about inheritance rights across religions, streedhan, and property rights after marriage or divorce.
