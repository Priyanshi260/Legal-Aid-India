Right to Information (RTI)

Understand how to use India's Right to Information Act, 2005 to seek accountability from public authorities. Learn how to file RTI applications, appeal decisions, and protect yourself as an applicant.

Articles & Guides
Article 01

RTI Act, 2005 — Overview

The Right to Information Act empowers every citizen to request information from any public authority. Learn what the law covers, who it applies to, and the key rights it creates.

Article 02

How to File an RTI Application

A step-by-step guide to drafting and submitting an RTI application — online via the RTI portal or offline by post — including fees, format, and what to write.

Article 03

What Information Can You Request?

Not all information is available under RTI. Understand what falls under the Act, what is exempt, and the difference between personal information and public interest disclosures.

Article 04

RTI Appeals — First & Second Appeal

If your RTI is rejected or ignored, you can appeal. Learn the two-tier appeal process — First Appellate Authority and the Central/State Information Commission — and the timelines involved.

Article 05

Exemptions Under RTI

Section 8 lists categories of information exempt from disclosure — including national security, Cabinet papers, and personal privacy. Understand when authorities can legally refuse your RTI.

Article 06

Penalties for Non-Compliance

Public Information Officers (PIOs) who fail to respond or provide false information face penalties. Learn about fines, disciplinary action, and compensation orders under the RTI Act.

Article 07

RTI for BPL Applicants — Fee Waiver

Citizens living below the poverty line (BPL) are exempt from paying RTI application fees. Learn how to claim this exemption and what documents are needed.

Article 08

RTI & Private Bodies

The RTI Act primarily covers public authorities. Understand when private organisations — like NGOs receiving government funding — fall under its ambit and how to seek information from them.

Article 09

Protecting RTI Applicants & Whistleblowers

RTI applicants and whistleblowers can face harassment. Learn about protections available under the Whistle Blowers Protection Act, 2014 and what to do if you face retaliation.
